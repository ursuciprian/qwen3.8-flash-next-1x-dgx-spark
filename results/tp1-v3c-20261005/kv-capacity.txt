KV capacity of the single-Spark pools (opus-v3c, 2026-10-05)

Serve-log lines (vLLM "GPU KV cache size"):
  v3b, 6 GiB pool            379,362 tokens   (results/lib-bench-20261005/v3b boot)
  v3c, 14 GiB pool           993,754 tokens   (results/tp1-v3c-20261005/bench boot)
  v3c, 16 GiB pool         1,135,812 tokens   (k41 screen, v3c-kv16 arm boot)

Pages: the hybrid KV pool is cut into pages of ~3.14 MiB (6 GiB = 1,958 pages, so 14 GiB = 4,568, 16 GiB = 5,221).
One page covers 3,024 tokens of one layer group. Per 3,024 tokens of context a request takes one page in each of
its 13 attention groups, plus a fixed number of GDN state pages: 37 with compact GDN records (74 at a boundary
step), 185 without (222 at a boundary step).

Requests that fit at once = pages / (13 x ceil((context + 512) / 3,024) + GDN pages):
  context   v3b 6 GiB (185)   v3c 14 GiB (37)   v3c 16 GiB (37)
  16K        1958/263 = 7.4    4568/115 = 39.7   5221/115 = 45.4
  64K        1958/471 = 4.2    4568/323 = 14.1   5221/323 = 16.2
  128K       1958/757 = 2.6    4568/609 =  7.5   5221/609 =  8.6
max_num_seqs is 8 in every recipe.
